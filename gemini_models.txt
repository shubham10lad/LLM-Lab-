
Gemini Models Information

Gemini is a family of AI models developed by Google.

Gemini Pro:
A model category intended for complex reasoning, coding,
content generation, and other demanding tasks.

Gemini Flash:
A model category intended for fast responses and efficient
processing, suitable for many everyday AI applications.

Gemini Flash-Lite:
A model category intended for lightweight, high-volume tasks
where speed and efficiency are important.

Exact model names and availability may change over time.
Always check official Google documentation for current details.
